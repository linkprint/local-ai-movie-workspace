# Style Blueprint

## Viral Evidence

The 1,039-vote Reddit post [Cunk on AI - Sam Altman - MiniMax H3](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1voeaef/cunk_on_ai_sam_altman_minimax_h3/) drew praise for short-form comic timing. The creator reports deliberately leaving silence before and after lines. This skill uses that timing lesson with fictional subjects and an original documentary voice rather than imitating a recognizable presenter.

## 10-Second Timing Budget

- 0.0–2.6: question.
- 2.6–4.5: silent processing and micro-reaction.
- 4.5–7.5: concise answer.
- 7.5–10.0: held reaction and room tone.

## 10-Second T2VA Demo

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Original deadpan mockumentary interview in a fluorescent municipal archive office. A fictional interviewer in a beige raincoat stands frame left holding a plain black microphone; a fictional middle-aged municipal cloud inspector in a gray cardigan sits frame right beside shelves of labeled but unreadable storage boxes. The camera is a slightly imperfect static shoulder-height two-shot with natural documentary exposure and no beauty lighting. The interviewer has a dry, measured voice (S1). At 00:00.500, she asks while looking directly at the guest: <d>[English] How often do you inspect the clouds?</d> Her lips then remain closed. The guest (S2) blinks once, inhales, glances toward the ceiling, and sits in complete silence for a deliberate beat while the interviewer holds the microphone steady. At 00:04.500, the guest answers in a calm administrative tone: <d>[English] Only when one files a complaint.</d> His mouth visibly matches only his own line. At 00:07.500, the camera pushes in with small amplitude at slow speed toward the interviewer as she lowers the microphone by two centimeters and stares at him without speaking. He quietly straightens one paper clip. End on the held awkward silence. No resemblance to real people, no named show, laughter track, subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, gibberish, or overlapping speech.

overall_soundscape: Constant fluorescent hum, distant ventilation, one chair creak, a quiet inhale, and a tiny metal paper-clip click. Preserve silence around the answer.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```
