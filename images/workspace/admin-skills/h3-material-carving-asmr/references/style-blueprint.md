# Material Carving ASMR Blueprint

## Technique

The viral construction is simple but continuity-sensitive: one recognizable source block, one original target sculpture, one stable adult pair of hands, and three cumulative carving stages. The material response carries the appeal. Do not use copyrighted anime characters, celebrities, living bodies, or injury imagery.

## Material Sheet

- Watermelon: wet red chips, pale rind layers, seed pockets, juice beads, crisp knife contacts.
- Floral foam: dry powder, porous crumble, soft scraping, compressed edges.
- Clay: ribbons, tacky drag, thumb compression, wire-tool curls.
- Wax or soap: translucent curls, clean facets, dry crisp shavings.
- Wood substitute: visible grain and curls only when tool handling can remain safe and plausible.

Choose one material and retain its behavior for the entire shot.

## Prompt Skeleton

integrated_multimodal_description: Macro craft ASMR on one fixed workbench. Two adult hands progressively carve one [MATERIAL] block into an original [SCULPTURE]. Keep the same block orientation, tool, hands, light, camera side, and removed-material pile. Stage one blocks the silhouette; stage two defines major forms; stage three adds surface details and cleans the reveal. Removed material never reappears. The tool travels away from fingers and never contacts skin.
