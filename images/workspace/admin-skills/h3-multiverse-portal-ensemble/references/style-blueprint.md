# Style Blueprint

## Viral Evidence

The 949-vote Reddit post [Assemble The Multiverse | Minimax H3 R2V is awesome!](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1vgf6qx/assemble_the_multiverse_minimax_h3_r2v_is_awesome/) shares subject definitions, identity retention, and sequential portal blocking for one- and three-character arrivals. This skill retains the staging method while requiring original characters rather than franchise identities.

## Reliable Arc

- Portal activates and establishes one color/effect system.
- Center subject arrives and clears the threshold.
- Two side subjects arrive one after another, each with one readable gesture.
- Camera makes one small pull-out and lands on a separated triangular formation.
