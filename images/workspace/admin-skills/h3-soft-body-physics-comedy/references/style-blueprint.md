# Style Blueprint

## Viral Evidence

The 951-vote Reddit post [Animals squeezing into jars (MiniMax H3)](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1vshijr/animals_squeezing_into_jars_minimax_h3/) became popular because the compression physics are “almost right” and remain visible against a hard glass constraint. A separate high-vote prompt discussion notes that H3 can assign different material physics to an object. This skill keeps the tactile experiment humane and fantastical.

## Reliable Arc

- Show the rigid opening and soft subject in the same frame.
- Assign material behavior before contact.
- Maintain volume and track the compression wave through the body.
- Show pressure release and return to the original form.
- Use an obviously comfortable, self-directed subject with no distress.
