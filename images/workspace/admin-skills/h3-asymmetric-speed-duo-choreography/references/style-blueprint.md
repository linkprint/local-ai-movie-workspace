# Asymmetric Speed Duo Blueprint

## Source and Adaptation

Technique adapted from [LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal Case 002](https://github.com/LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal/tree/77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd/case-studies/2026-08-dual-subject-speed-contrast), commit `77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd`, under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 LoveRain1997.

The reusable insight is numerical: “fast” and “slow” are ambiguous, while a 3:1 completed-action ratio is auditable. This adaptation uses original adult subjects, choreography, and music rather than the source's named characters or song reference.

## Ratio Ledger

```text
Cycle 1: A completes A1, A2, A3 while B watches and completes B1.
Cycle 2: A completes A4, A5, A6 while B imitates an earlier leader move as B2.
Cycle 3: A completes A7, A8, A9 while B completes B3.
Invariant: no catch-up, no synchronized phrase, no sudden follower acceleration.
```
