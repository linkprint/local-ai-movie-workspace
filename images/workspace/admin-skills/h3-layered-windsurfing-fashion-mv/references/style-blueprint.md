## Three-Segment Arc

- Segment 1 — entry and identity: establish face, costume, board, controlled wind, and the first acceleration.
- Segment 2 — equipment and speed: prove grip, rig geometry, sail tension, carving, spray, and a compact moving turnaround.
- Segment 3 — text-only climax: giant stylized wave, takeoff, airborne low-angle telephoto hero hold at the music peak, controlled landing, and a clean fashion finish.

Mirror the ending state of one segment in the opening description of the next, but do not claim pixel-perfect continuity or uninterrupted audio from independent renders.
