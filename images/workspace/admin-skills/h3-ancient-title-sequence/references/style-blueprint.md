# Ancient Title-Sequence Blueprint

## Technique

The useful construction is a 15-second historical-drama opening made from tactile period materials rather than a miniature plot: macro artifacts, ink and textile transformations, symbolic match cuts, a controlled two-color palette, and one final title lockup. Use a fictional period identity and original heraldry.

## Prompt Skeleton

integrated_multimodal_description: Create a [DURATION]-second, 16:9 opening-title sequence for the fictional historical drama “[TITLE]”. Governing palette: [COLOR 1] and [COLOR 2]. Repeating materials: [MATERIAL A], [MATERIAL B], [MATERIAL C]. Move through four symbolic stages: [ARTIFACT], [MAP OR WRITING SURFACE], [LANDSCAPE OR ARCHITECTURE], [EMBLEM AND TITLE]. Connect every stage through a shared contour and a physical transformation such as ink spreading, silk pulling, dust lifting, rain revealing relief, reflection, or firelight. Reserve open negative space and use only the exact title and optional subtitle. Hold the final title for at least one second.

overall_soundscape: Close material textures, restrained wind or water, distant architecture, and one emblem impact.

non_diegetic_music: Original restrained historical-drama cue with plucked strings, frame drum, low wood, and metal resonance.

## 10-Second T2VA Demo

integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Premium opening-title imagery for an original fictional river kingdom, rendered in mineral-black ink, oxidized jade green, warm ivory silk, and dark bronze. Extreme macro: a weathered bronze river seal rests on woven ivory silk as fine rain beads inside its carved channels. At 00:02.000 one droplet runs through the carving and becomes a black ink river on an old hand-painted map; the seal's circular border becomes the map's mountain contour in a precise graphic match. At 00:04.000 a silk ribbon pulls across the frame and the ink mountains rise into a misty dawn landscape of original wooden watchtowers and river bridges, no recognizable monument. At 00:06.500 bronze arrowheads planted in wet earth align into a fictional sun-and-river emblem seen from above. Wind lifts dark mineral dust through the emblem, revealing a final ivory title field with one large centered abstract seal-mark and non-readable charcoal title bars. Hold the balanced final composition from 00:08.500 to 00:10.000. Tactile material continuity, restrained camera, no battles, no modern objects, no named dynasty, no readable text, logos, subtitles, watermarks, adult content, or graphic gore.

overall_soundscape: Rain on bronze, silk pull, wet ink spread, distant wood creaks, low wind, arrowhead contact, and one deep seal impact.

non_diegetic_music: Original sparse plucked-string and frame-drum motif with breathy wood tones and a final low bronze resonance.
