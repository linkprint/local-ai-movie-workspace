# Style Blueprint

## Viral Evidence

The technique was distilled from the 3,238-vote Reddit post [Spaghetti eating Will Smith - MiniMax H3](https://www.reddit.com/r/StableDiffusion/comments/1ve4ja4/spaghetti_eating_will_smith_minimax_h3/). The shared prompt succeeds by defining an impossible subject with literal negative anatomy, maintaining miniature scale, describing liquid and utensil physics, and using a macro-to-reveal camera path. Reuse the method, never the celebrity or the scene.

## Reliable Arc

- 0–35%: calm macro setup from the tiny subject's scale.
- 35–60%: one large object enters and physically disrupts the environment.
- 60–100%: the camera follows the disruption into a larger uncanny reveal and ends on the payoff.
- Repeat scale anchors and material rules where the camera changes scale.

## 10-Second T2VA Demo

```text
integrated_multimodal_description: [Shot 1] Surreal photorealistic absurdist comedy with premium practical-effects realism. At extreme macro scale inside a porcelain teacup, six tiny original raincoat-clad commuters stand on a floating lemon slice as if it were a ferry. Warm café light reflects through amber tea; steam towers above them like fog. The camera tracks backward just above the liquid while the lemon slice rocks with realistic buoyancy and the tiny commuters balance against its motion. At 00:03.500, an enormous silver teaspoon descends into the tea, displacing a broad wave that tilts the lemon ferry and makes the commuters crouch and grab the rind. The spoon slides underneath the lemon slice. Without a cut, the camera rises beside the spoon as tea streams from its edge. At 00:06.000, the larger café table is revealed: the cup belongs to a gentle living teapot made entirely from folded white paper, with no human skin, face, hands, or limbs; two folded paper strips manipulate the spoon. A narrow origami flap opens in the teapot body, the spoon tips the lemon ferry safely onto a dry paper platform inside, and the flap closes. The tiny commuters remain the same size and never transform. End immediately on one commuter adjusting a yellow hood and looking relieved. No subtitles, captions, logos, watermarks, UI, or visible text.

overall_soundscape: Close liquid ambience, lemon rind creaks, tiny rubber soles, a broad tea splash, metal dripping, porcelain clink, and crisp folded-paper rustle. No dialogue.

non_diegetic_music: N/A
```
