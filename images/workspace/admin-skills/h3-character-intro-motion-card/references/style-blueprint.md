# Character Intro Motion-Card Blueprint

## Source and Technique

This workflow was selected from Koda's X post, “MiniMax H3 Character Introduction Template.” When reviewed, the post showed about 167.1K views, 1.1K likes, and 1.3K bookmarks. The public prompt uses replaceable text parameters, one character sheet as the sole identity/style authority, exactly 13 cuts, alternating cinematic action and bold editorial typography, then a long final identity card.

The reference image shown with the prompt is an airy landscape character concept board, not a cinematic keyframe. Its useful layout is:

- warm ivory paper with generous negative space;
- one large full-body neutral hero near center-left;
- four small orthographic turnarounds across the upper-right;
- three dark silhouette/readability studies at mid-left;
- multiple seated, action, and rear poses down the right;
- five face and expression crops along the lower-left;
- narrow material, accessory, eye, and construction-detail strips along the lower edge;
- elegant black serif display title, tiny handwritten-style notes, fine rules, and restrained annotation;
- mostly monochrome clothing with a small controlled accent palette.

Use this information architecture, not the source character, name, clothing, or exact board design.

## Reference-Sheet Generation Prompt

Create a 16:9 premium editorial character concept board on warm off-white archival paper for one original adult [CHARACTER ARCHETYPE]. Large central full-body neutral standing view at center-left, fully visible from head to shoes. Four small orthographic turnaround views across the upper-right: front, three-quarter, side, rear. Three compact black silhouette studies at mid-left. On the right, include two seated poses, two action poses, one rear detail pose, and one prop interaction. Along the lower-left, include five consistent face-expression close-ups. Along the lower edge, include cropped construction details for eyes, hair, fabric, fasteners, footwear, signature accessory, and prop mechanism. Every inset depicts the exact same adult person with identical facial structure, body proportions, hairstyle, outfit topology, accessories, material finishes, and color palette. Airy museum-catalog composition, fine black serif display type used sparingly, tiny handwritten annotation texture, thin guide rules, muted beige paper, restrained [PALETTE], polished semi-realistic anime concept art, premium fashion-design presentation, crisp linework, subtle material rendering. No alternate costumes, no extra characters, no exposed anatomy, no sexualized poses, no named IP, no logos, no watermark. Annotation text need not be readable.

Approve this sheet before using it as the H3 character authority. The full-body view must be large enough to read at 480p, and all insets must agree.

## Full 15-Second Ref2VA Template

Use Image 1 as the sole authority for the character's identity, body proportions, face, hairstyle, outfit topology, accessories, materials, colors, and visual rendering style. Never redesign the character. Adapt environment, abilities, VFX, and movement naturally to the approved concept board.

Create a 15-second premium cinematic original-character reveal with exactly 13 distinct cuts at 24fps. Motion-design sections use oversized condensed typography, clean white or pale-grey fields, huge circular color blocks, thin technical diagrams, concentric rings, halftone, tiny interface markings, scan lines, geometric accents, sweeping brush or energy strokes, and strong foreground/background parallax. Use black and white plus a three-color accent palette derived from the character sheet. In every text card, show the exact supplied typography clearly first; only then may the character, crop, or silhouette overlap it.

1. 0.00–1.10 FACE: extreme cinematic face close-up, calm focus, natural partial occlusion, shallow depth of field, subtle push-in.
2. 1.10–2.20 ACTIVATION: one character-specific effect ignites around eyes, hands, body, or equipment; gaze lifts and effect peaks.
3. 2.20–3.20 ACTIVE CARD: hard graphic match to bright editorial freeze frame; large circle, condensed type, technical rings, halftone, sharp diagonal streaks. Show STATUS_TEXT_1.
4. 3.20–4.10 ENGAGE CARD: high-contrast silhouette over an accent-color typography band; luminous brush or energy ribbon crosses the foreground. Show STATUS_TEXT_2.
5. 4.10–5.20 WORLD: smash cut to a character-suited original environment; immediate dash, leap, land, or burst with reactive tracking.
6. 5.20–6.20 DETAIL: close or medium view of one defining original ability, prop motion, gesture, transformation, or acrobatic action.
7. 6.20–6.90 INTERSTITIAL 1: text-led graphic screen with circles, technical lines, scan lines, halftone, data texture, and aggressive wipes. Show INTERSTITIAL_TEXT_1.
8. 6.90–7.90 IMPACT: return to action from a new aggressive angle; one attack, dodge, release, or interaction with a simple abstract threat.
9. 7.90–8.60 INTERSTITIAL 2: cleaner punchier editorial card with diagonal slashes, accent bars, fine UI, and strong parallax. Show INTERSTITIAL_TEXT_2.
10. 8.60–9.60 VELOCITY: character crosses close to lens, launches, or changes direction while the environment stretches into controlled speed blur.
11. 9.60–10.30 INTERSTITIAL 3: oversized type, energy stroke, geometric overlays, concentric diagrams, and fast silhouette insert. Show INTERSTITIAL_TEXT_3.
12. 10.30–12.30 HERO MOMENT: the most iconic original movement, ability, landing, or confident stop; stabilize briefly at the peak, then graphic-match out.
13. 12.30–15.00 CHARACTER ID: pale background, enormous translucent rings, fine arcs, nodes, halftone, tiny markings, and restrained chromatic glints. Full-body hero overlaps CHARACTER_NAME after the name first registers clearly. Add SUBTITLE and optional PROJECT_TEXT. Behind the hero, place one large black circular brush emblem with a ghosted original identity motif.

Editing: hard cuts, graphic matches, whip pans, freeze frames, impact flashes, directional smears, foreground wipes, silhouette transitions, and short speed ramps. Every cut uses a clearly different composition. No slow dissolves or uninterrupted combat coverage.

overall_soundscape: UI ticks, typography hits, whooshes, character-specific ability textures, energy swells, cloth and gear movement, sub impacts, and a final identity-card stinger.

non_diegetic_music: Original fast character-driven premium action-game cue, adapted to the hero, building from restrained tension to punchy percussion and bass, peaking at the hero moment and finishing with a final reveal hit. No existing song references.
