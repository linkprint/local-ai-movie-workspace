# Occlusion Orbit Ensemble Blueprint

## Source and Technique

This workflow was distilled from AI Dance Paradise's X post about a three-person MiniMax H3 tracking shot. When reviewed, the post showed about 89.4K views, 1.4K likes, and 1.1K bookmarks. The author described the core construction directly: each subject receives a close-range orbit from feet to head and toward the side-rear; the camera never stops or cuts; the current body becomes a full-frame foreground occluder and the next subject is discovered behind it.

Use that spatial handoff system with original adult subjects.

## Imported Case Technique: Three-Person Orbital Long Take

Technique adapted from [LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal Case 001](https://github.com/LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal/tree/77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd/case-studies/2026-08-three-person-orbital-long-take), commit `77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd`, under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 LoveRain1997.

Its layered reference map is:

- Picture 1: Subject A plus master environment, floor plane, lighting, and spatial system.
- Picture 2: Subject B identity only.
- Picture 3: Subject C identity only.

Each subject receives a complete orbit vocabulary: feet-to-head vertical rise, then frontal, three-quarter, side, and rear-side horizontal coverage. During the first three sections of a subject's phrase, no other person is visible. The body or costume then fills the lens as a real foreground occluder; unchanged velocity discovers the next subject behind it. Only the closing half-body reveal contains all three people.

The original 20-second plan exceeds the current single-job 15-second limit. Preserve it as a prompt-only or explicitly authorized chained plan. For a native 15-second compression, allocate roughly four seconds per subject and three seconds to handoffs plus the group hold; do not delete the rear-side coverage merely to fit more poses.

## Stage Map

- Place A front-left, B rear-center, and C front-right on one visible floor plane.
- A phrase: shoes to face to left shoulder, then full-frame coat occlusion.
- B phrase: lower silhouette to face to right side-rear, then full-frame fabric occlusion.
- C phrase: feet to face to rear shoulder, then reveal the whole trio or return to A.
- Keep camera velocity, direction, lens, exposure, and height continuous through the dark occlusion frames.
