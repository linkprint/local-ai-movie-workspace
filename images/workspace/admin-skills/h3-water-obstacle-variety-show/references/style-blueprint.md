# Water Obstacle Variety Show Blueprint

## Source and Adaptation

Technique adapted from [LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal Case 005](https://github.com/LoveRain1997/h3-prompt-journal/tree/77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd/case-studies/2026-08-water-obstacle-variety-show), commit `77f1fa7997c9ed9daa2e20ab921a797d2e66babd`, under the MIT License, Copyright (c) 2026 LoveRain1997.

The useful method is beat-anchored improvisation: lock a few memorable outcomes while giving the model room to invent safe connective action, crowd response, and broadcast framing. This adaptation makes the contestant an adult and strengthens padding, supervision, and no-injury constraints.

## Full Beat Skeleton

1. Rolling cylinder crossed successfully.
2. Fishbone-style padded obstacle causes a harmless slip.
3. Contestant immediately recovers and continues.
4. Contestant grabs the final wall edge and appears safe.
5. One slow padded mechanical arm taps the contestant sideways.
6. Clean feet-first or side-body splash into shallow supervised water.
7. Contestant surfaces safely, hair wet, and delivers one dry punchline.
